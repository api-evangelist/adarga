When we built our first Natural Language Processing pipeline at Adarga in 2016, we did so on a simple premise: allied Defence, Security and Resilience (DSR) organisations would only trust AI they could hold to account. A decade on, that premise still shapes everything we build, from Catalyst to our mission applications and agents. That approach has now been independently assured.
